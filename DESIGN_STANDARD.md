# PANIT UI standard

Apply this design standard when changing this repository's customer and partner screens.

- Brand: navy #101d36, lime #c5ee56 for primary accents, blue #315be8 for focus and links; white backgrounds and muted text #536176.
- Use a Korean system font stack. Body 16px with line height 1.65. Keep mobile form text at least 16px.
- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32px. Controls radius 10px, cards and dialogs 16px.
- Primary actions at least 48px high, secondary controls at least 44px. Keep keyboard focus visible. Label all inputs and icon buttons.
- Preserve responsive desktop and mobile layouts, safe-area bottom padding, PC-view switch, and existing navigation destinations.
- Product cards share image proportions, content padding, price hierarchy and action placement. Use licensed or clearly identified conceptual images; never fabricate endorsements or artist availability.
- Multiple services belong in one event consultation basket. Quantities and per-item desired budgets must be separate from the overall event budget. Do not claim reservation, payment or successful submission until real backend confirmation exists.
- Brand setup price: KRW150,000 plus VAT, total KRW165,000. Preserve proposal/contract qualifications for commission rates.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences. Check keyboard operation, empty states, narrow screens, and content overflow after UI edits.
